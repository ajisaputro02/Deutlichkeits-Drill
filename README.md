## 🎯 What Is KlangSchmiede?

KlangSchmiede is a spoken-clarity companion for people who want their everyday speech to land with purpose. Where AusspracheTrainer focused on helping practitioners sharpen their diction in daily life, KlangSchmiede takes the same philosophy and forges it into a workshop-like environment — a smithy for syllables, a tuning bench for tone. The name says it: we hammer, we polish, we listen, we refine.

This repository hosts the full source tree for the KlangSchmiede ecosystem: the desktop-grade training studio, the lightweight mobile companion, the speech-analytics engine, and the coaching dashboard that ties it all together. Whether you are a speech therapist, a language coach, an actor rehearsing tongue-twisters at midnight, or simply someone who wants to be understood the first time they speak — KlangSchmiede was built with you in mind.

The project was born from a simple observation: most pronunciation tools treat the voice like a checkbox. Say the word, get a green tick, move on. KlangSchmiede treats the voice like an instrument — one that deserves patience, warmth, and the occasional oiling of its hinges. Every feature here exists to serve that metaphor.

## ✨ Why KlangSchmiede Exists

Communication is not a transaction. It is a craft. And like any craft, it rewards deliberate practice. KlangSchmiede gives you the anvil, the hammer, and the quiet room. What you forge is up to you.

We built this because:
- Everyday clarity should not be a luxury reserved for trained broadcasters.
- Feedback should be kind, specific, and immediate.
- Progress should be visible without being judgmental.
- Practice should fit into the cracks of a busy day, not demand a monastery.

## 🚀 Feature Constellation

### 🎙️ Real-Time Articulation Mirror
Speak into your device and watch a live visualization of your vowel shapes, consonant crispness, and pacing rhythm. Not a score — a mirror. You decide what to adjust.

### 🧭 Adaptive Drill Paths
The engine studies your patterns across sessions and gently reshuffles the practice queue. If you consistently blur the "sch" in "Schule," you will meet it again — but framed differently each time, so boredom never sets in. This is machine-guided, human-approved.

### 🌍 Multilingual Support
KlangSchmiede speaks eighteen languages at launch, including German, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish, Arabic, Mandarin, and Hindi. Each locale ships with native-speaker reference recordings and culture-aware drill sets. Language is not a barrier here — it is the material we work with.

### 📱 Responsive UI Across Every Screen
The interface folds itself gracefully from a 32-inch desktop monitor down to a 5-inch phone display. Touch targets grow on mobile. Panels collapse on tablets. Nothing is ever clipped, hidden, or unreachable. Responsive is not a feature — it is a promise.

### 🕰️ Around-the-Clock Support
Questions at 3 a.m.? A drill that refuses to cooperate on a Sunday morning? Our support crew rotates through every time zone so that a human replies within hours, not business days. This is what we mean by 24/7 customer support — real people, real answers.

### 📊 Progress Cartography
See your speech journey as a topographic map. Plateaus are visible. Breakthroughs leave trails. You can export your map as a printable chart or share it with a coach.

### 🔒 Local-First Privacy
Audio is processed on your device by default. Nothing leaves the machine unless you explicitly opt into cloud sync. Your voice is yours. Full stop.

### 🧩 Plugin Surface for Coaches
Speech therapists and language instructors can author custom drill packs using a simple declarative format. No compiler degree required.

### 🎨 Theming & Accessibility
High-contrast mode, dyslexia-friendly typography, adjustable animation speed, screen-reader narration of every panel. Accessibility is not a checkbox here — it is a design north star.

## 🧠 The Philosophy Behind the Code

KlangSchmiede is not a productivity app. It is not a gamified streak-chaser. It is closer to a musical instrument that happens to live on your phone. You do not "complete" it. You return to it. You notice new things each time. The codebase reflects that ethos: we favor slow, well-tested modules over quick hacks, and we write documentation as carefully as we write logic.

The architecture is split into four cooperating realms:

- **Resonanz** — the audio capture and analysis core.
- **Werkbank** — the drill authoring and scheduling engine.
- **Karte** — the progress visualization layer.
- **Bühne** — the user interface shell, shared across desktop and mobile.

Each realm communicates through a narrow, well-documented interface. This makes the whole system easier to reason about, easier to test, and easier to extend without fear.

## 🛠️ Getting Started (Without the Usual Ritual)

We deliberately avoid the standard incantations here. KlangSchmiede is distributed as a self-contained bundle for each supported platform. Fetch the release package that matches your operating system, unpack it into a folder you trust, and launch the executable inside. That is the whole ceremony.

For developers who wish to build from source, the project expects a modern runtime environment. Consult the `docs/build.md` file inside the repository for the toolchain expectations. We do not list them here because they shift with each major release, and stale instructions are worse than no instructions.

Once running, you will be greeted by a short guided tour. It takes about four minutes. You can skip it, but we recommend letting it finish — it explains the metaphor better than any README ever could.

## 🔐 Privacy, Trust, and Data Handling

We treat your voice recordings with the same care a luthier treats a Stradivarius. By default, all audio analysis happens on your device. Cloud features are opt-in, clearly labeled, and can be revoked at any time. We do not sell data. We do not train third-party models on your recordings. We do not have hidden analytics that phone home. If you want to know exactly what leaves your machine, read `docs/privacy.md` — it is written in plain language, not legalese.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a microphone beyond my phone or laptop?**
No. Built-in microphones work well for daily practice. External microphones improve fidelity but are never required.

**Can I use KlangSchmiede without an internet connection?**
Yes. The core training loop runs entirely offline. Network access is only needed for optional sync and support conversations.

**Is this a replacement for a speech therapist?**
No, and we would never claim that. KlangSchmiede is a practice companion. It supports professional care; it does not substitute for it.

**Which languages are supported right now?**
Eighteen at launch, with more arriving throughout 2026. See `docs/localization.md` for the living list.

**How is progress measured?**
Through pattern recognition across sessions, not through a single numeric score. The map matters more than the number.
